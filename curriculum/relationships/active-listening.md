# Active Listening

The most underrated relationship skill. Most people listen to reply, not to understand.

## What Active Listening Looks Like

1. **Give full attention** — Put your phone down, make eye contact, face the person
2. **Don't interrupt** — Let them finish their thought, even if you disagree
3. **Reflect back** — "So what I'm hearing is..." shows you're processing
4. **Ask questions** — "How did that make you feel?" or "What happened next?"
5. **Validate** — "That makes sense" or "I can see why you'd feel that way"

## What NOT to Do

- Don't try to fix the problem unless asked
- Don't compare ("That's nothing, listen to what happened to me...")
- Don't judge or lecture
- Don't rehearse your response while they're talking

## Practice Exercise

This week, have one conversation where your only goal is to understand the other person. Don't offer advice unless they ask. Notice how the conversation feels different.
