# How to Cook 5 Essential Meals

Cooking is a life skill every man should have. It saves money, keeps you healthy, and builds confidence. Here are five meals to master.

## 1. Perfect Scrambled Eggs

**Time:** 5 minutes

1. Crack 3 eggs into a bowl and whisk until uniform
2. Heat a non-stick pan on medium-low with a pat of butter
3. Pour in eggs and stir gently with a rubber spatula
4. Remove from heat just before they look done (carryover cooking finishes them)
5. Season with salt, pepper, and fresh chives

## 2. One-Pan Chicken and Vegetables

**Time:** 30 minutes

1. Season chicken thighs with salt, pepper, and olive oil
2. Chop vegetables (broccoli, bell peppers, carrots)
3. Place everything on a sheet pan
4. Roast at 400°F for 25 minutes
5. Squeeze lemon over the top before serving

## 3. Simple Pasta Aglio e Olio

**Time:** 20 minutes

1. Boil water and cook 200g of spaghetti
2. While pasta cooks, thinly slice 4 cloves of garlic
3. Heat 1/4 cup olive oil in a pan, add garlic
4. When garlic is golden, add red pepper flakes
5. Drain pasta, reserve 1/2 cup pasta water
6. Toss pasta with garlic oil, add pasta water as needed
7. Finish with parsley and Parmesan

## 4. Basic Stir Fry

**Time:** 15 minutes

1. Cook rice according to package directions
2. Slice protein (chicken, beef, or tofu) into thin strips
3. Stir-fry in a hot pan with oil for 3-4 minutes
4. Add chopped vegetables (broccoli, bell pepper, snap peas)
5. Add sauce (soy sauce, ginger, garlic, a touch of honey)
6. Serve over rice

## 5. Overnight Oats

**Time:** 5 minutes prep

1. Combine 1/2 cup rolled oats, 1/2 cup milk, 1 tbsp chia seeds
2. Add a pinch of salt and a drizzle of maple syrup
3. Stir, cover, and refrigerate overnight
4. Top with berries, banana, and nuts in the morning
