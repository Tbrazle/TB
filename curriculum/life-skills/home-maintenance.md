# Basic Home Maintenance Every Man Should Know

Taking care of your home prevents expensive repairs and builds self-reliance.

## Unclogging a Drain

1. Remove standing water if possible
2. Try boiling water first (for metal pipes only)
3. Use a plunger to create suction
4. If that fails, try a mixture of 1/2 cup baking soda + 1/2 cup vinegar
5. Wait 15 minutes, flush with hot water
6. As a last resort, use a drain snake

## Fixing a Running Toilet

1. Open the tank and check the flapper valve
2. If it's worn or misaligned, replace it ($5 at hardware store)
3. Adjust the chain so there's slight slack
4. Check the fill valve — if water is overflowing the tube, adjust the float

## Changing a Tire

1. Find a flat, stable surface away from traffic
2. Apply parking brake and place wheel wedges
3. Loosen lug nuts slightly before jacking up the car
4. Jack up the car, remove lug nuts, replace tire
5. Tighten lug nuts in a star pattern
6. Lower the car and torque again

## Resetting a Circuit Breaker

1. Unplug devices from the affected room
2. Open the breaker panel
3. Locate the tripped breaker (it will be in the middle position)
4. Flip it fully to OFF, then back to ON
5. Test the room — if it trips again, call an electrician
