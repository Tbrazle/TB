# Being Present With Your Child

Presence > presents. Kids don't remember the toys as much as they remember feeling seen.

## Small Moments Matter

- **Morning:** Sit with them for 5 minutes before looking at your phone
- **After work:** Spend 10 minutes doing whatever they want before starting chores
- **Bedtime:** Read a story or just talk about their day

## The Art of Listening to Kids

1. Get down to their eye level
2. Ask open-ended questions (not just "how was school?")
3. Try: "What made you laugh today?" or "What was hard today?"
4. Don't jump to problem-solving — sometimes they just need to vent

## Modeling Emotions

Your kids learn emotional regulation from watching you:
- Say "I'm feeling frustrated right now, so I'm going to take a deep breath"
- Apologize when you lose your cool
- Show affection freely — hugs, high-fives, "I love you"

## One Challenge

This week, spend 15 minutes of completely device-free time with your child. Let them choose the activity. Just be there.
